**What works**
- **Scenic opener in the first second.** The Mykonos beach & Little Venice reels kept viewers best (lowest skip, 11–13 s average watch).
- **Big emotional/community moments.** "A special day with family and friends" (509 views), anniversary (4 comments), Onam & Diwali get the most comments.
- **Cars.** The car reel did 4–5× your normal reach — worth one deliberate follow-up to test whether it was a fluke.
- **Guide-style captions** ("8 favourite spots… save this") — the only format earning saves.

**What's holding you back**
1. **Hooks.** 2 in 3 viewers swipe away within 3 s. The first frame is often a walk-in or a slow pan.
2. **Off-niche posts underperform.** Gym motivation (avg ~200 views, 1–9 likes), home décor (75 views), night drive (46). They confuse the algorithm about who to show you to.
3. **Batch dumping.** Several days had 2–3 reels within hours (11 Oct, 14 Jun, 21 Jun, 27 Jun). Each one steals reach from the others.
4. **Yesterday's Diwali reel has no caption** — the caption was posted as a comment instead. Captions are searchable; comments aren't. **Fix:** open the reel → ⋯ → Edit → paste the caption from the comment, then delete the comment.
5. **Comments go unanswered** — replies in the first hour are one of the strongest signals you control.

## 2. The rules from now on

1. **One reel a day, max. 4–5 a week.** Space the backlog out instead of dumping it.
2. **Stay in lane — 4 content pillars:**
   - 🌍 **Family travel guides** (Europe trips) — "X spots / costs / tips with a toddler"
   - 🇬🇧 **UK weekend trips** — day trips, villages, seasonal events
   - 🌴 **Malayali life in the UK** — festivals, community, food, culture
   - 👶 **Dad & son moments** — milestones, firsts
   - Gym/home/car-only content → Stories, not the grid (except one car test).
3. **First 1 second = the best shot + text on screen.** E.g. open on the turquoise water, *then* the walk.
4. **Every caption:** hook line → 1–2 lines of story/value → one question → 5–8 specific hashtags (always `#UKMallu`).
5. **Reply to every comment within the first hour** of posting.
6. **Every travel reel ends with "Full film on YouTube — link in bio"** to push The roaming lens.
