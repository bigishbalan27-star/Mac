# References

Videos whose style you like — from any creator, brand, or platform.

**What goes here**
- Video files (or a `links.md` with URLs) of edits you want to learn from
- Optional: a short note per video on *what* you like (pacing, captions, transitions, color, music, hooks)

**How it's used:** These are studied to pick up the look and feel you're after. They are inspiration only, never copied directly into your edits.
