# made-by-claude_v1_9x16

**Idea:** a reel that explains how it was made. It takes the "Claude makes reels for me" idea from reference VID_20261117_090035 and keeps every claim true to how this video was actually built.

- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 41.3 s, H.264 + AAC, loudness normalised to −14 LUFS
- **Style taken from all three references:** off-white grid canvas; kinetic captions word by word, with key words bold and emphasis words in blue serif italic; one central illustration that builds on every phrase; a new visual about every 1.5 s; and a "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send…" ending
- **Added on top of the references:** a chapter tracker (WATCH · WRITE · VOICE · BUILD · FIX · SHIP), a dark "best part" act with a circular reveal back to light, and a sound design layer (whooshes, pops, typing clicks)
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs "Kyle – Bright, Active, Commercial" (eleven_v4), take 2 of 2
- **Caption timing:** word timestamps from faster-whisper run on the voiceover itself
- **Built with:** Remotion 4 (source in `made-by-claude_source/`)
