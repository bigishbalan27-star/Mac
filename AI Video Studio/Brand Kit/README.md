# Brand Kit

Your visual and audio brand assets.

**What goes here**
- Logos (PNG with transparent background, SVG if available)
- Brand colors (hex codes in a `colors.md`)
- Fonts (font files or names)
- Intro/outro clips, lower thirds, watermarks, end screens
- Music and sound effects you own or have licensed

**How it's used:** These assets are pulled into every edit so videos look consistently on-brand.
