# Editing Style

Your written rules for how videos should be edited.

**What goes here**
- A style guide (e.g. `style-guide.md`): pacing, cut frequency, caption font/size/position, zooms, transitions, B-roll use, music volume, sound effects, intro/outro format, aspect ratios per platform
- Do's and don'ts ("never use star wipes", "always cut silences over 0.5s")
- Notes from feedback on past edits

**How it's used:** Every edit follows these rules. Keep it up to date as your preferences change.
