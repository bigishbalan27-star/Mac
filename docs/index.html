What the editor learns from.

Four folders describe your style. One order is fixed: your approved work is matched before references.

Editing Style

Written rules apply to every edit.

Pacing, cut frequency, caption font and position, music volume, aspect ratios per platform. Plus plain do's and don'ts, such as “never use star wipes” or “always cut silences over 0.5s”.

Approved Videos

Your own finished work is matched first.

Every edit you sign off becomes the best example of your style. New edits are matched to these before anything in References.

References

Other people's videos come after your own.

They show the look and feel you are after: pacing, captions, transitions, colour, music, hooks. They are inspiration only and are never copied directly into your edits.

Brand Kit

Your assets go into every edit.

Logos, brand colours, fonts, intro and outro clips, lower thirds, and music you own or have licensed.

Every job starts with a notes.md.

Each subfolder in Raw/ is one video. Its notes.md answers five things before any cutting starts.

  • TargetThe platform and the length.
  • HookThe key message or hook.
  • Must keepThe moments that have to stay in.
  • CutAnything that has to go.
  • DeadlineWhen the final version is due.

Drafts name themselves.

Every export carries the name of its job, a version, and the platform it was cut for, so nothing in Exports/ needs explaining.

podcast-ep12Matches its folder in Raw.
_v2The version number. Each new draft counts up.
_9x16The platform cut: _9x16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, _16x9 for YouTube.
.mp4The rendered file.

An optional feedback.md beside the drafts holds your notes on each one.

Copy the board.

  1. Take the folders. Open the repository and download or fork the AI Video Studio folder.
  2. Write your rules. Put a style guide in Editing Style/ and your assets in Brand Kit/.
  3. Start a job. Make a subfolder in Raw/, add your footage and a notes.md.
