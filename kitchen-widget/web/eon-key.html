Connect your E.ON meter

Do this once. Your key goes straight from E.ON's website into the kitchen board on this Mac.

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: press ⇧ Shift + ⌘ Cmd + B.
  2. Drag this orange button up onto the bookmarks bar:
    ⚡ Get E.ON key
    Drag it, don't click it here.
  3. Go to www.eonnext.com and sign in, until you see your account.
  4. On that E.ON page, click ⚡ Get E.ON key in the bookmarks bar. Wait a few seconds for the message "Done".
