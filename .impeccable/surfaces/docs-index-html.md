# Surface brief: landing page

Scope: `docs/index.html`, the single landing page for AI Video Studio. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job (inferred; the owner answered "no preference" to every interview question): solo creators who edit with AI tools, deciding whether to copy this workspace. Action: open the public GitHub repository. Proof and content: the six real folders, the real workflow and naming rules from the READMEs. Constraints: no invented videos, results, prices, customers or contact details. Example job data comes from the READMEs' own examples and is labelled as an example.

Memorable moment: a job strip that moves from Raw to Exports to Approved Videos as the visitor advances it.

Unresolved: who the page is really for and what the action should be were never confirmed by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a film production stripboard. It owns the idea that a video workflow is a board of labelled strips you can read at a glance. It refuses the dark gradient product page with a glowing timeline mock-up.

OWN-WORLD: Matte card strips on a warm black vinyl board with thin aluminium rails. Six card colours, one per folder: blue, green, white, pink, yellow, orange. Black break strips with white capitals divide groups. Red is reserved for the action. Extra-condensed capitals on strips, a plain humanist sans for reading text. Cells divided by hairline rules, like a strip's fields.

STORY: The visitor sees six folders, understands that four teach the editor a style and two carry each job, watches one job move through, and opens the repository to copy it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A black break strip as navigation. Under it a tall white header strip spanning the full width, carrying the headline in extra-condensed capitals at up to 6rem, a short explanation (three sentences, kept because they carry the four-folders-for-style, two-for-jobs offer), and the red action at its right end. Directly below, the six folder strips stacked full width, split by two break strips into "style" and "jobs", all visible at 1440 by 900.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: One job strip slides between three lanes (Raw, Exports, Approved Videos) through five steps, using view transitions, with a single button that names each next action. MOTION GRAMMAR (widened at the owner's request for more animation): strips are dealt onto the board on load; each later section deals, drops or rises its strips in once as it scrolls into view; headings rise through a clip; the job strip plays its five steps by itself once, with a red progress line, until the visitor presses the button. All exponential ease-out, all off under reduced motion.

FORM: production stripboard, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:0c80755ea818f5a6. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this session). The roll ran degraded: no challengers, no quality-bar boards.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
